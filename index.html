🏛️ College Tycoon

Five years. Five departments. One campus to build.

You have just been appointed to run a private college with five departments: College (diploma & degree), Vocational Education (TVET), Corporate Training (industry clients), Marketing (enquiries and brand), and Education STEM (school outreach). Every month you choose where the money goes. After sixty months the board scores what you built.

How to play

Everything compounds. Nothing is free.

  • Marketing generates enquiries; reputation and conversion turn them into enrolments. Enrolments are capped by seats, so expand before you advertise.
  • College and Vocational departments carry the recurring fee income. Their quality depends on the learner-to-staff ratio — grow enrolment without hiring and quality, reputation and retention all slide.
  • Corporate Training converts industry partners into high-margin programmes. You can only deliver as many programmes as your trainers can staff.
  • Education STEM earns little directly, but feeds the applicant pipeline and builds the community goodwill that lifts reputation.
  • Compliance decays every month. Let it fall and the regulator will find you.
  • Funding modes (Lean → Max) trade cash for output and staff morale. Lean is a real option when the runway is short — just not for long.
  • The board reviews you every 12 months and may release a development grant.
  • You lose if cash falls below −RM 300,000 or reputation collapses.
